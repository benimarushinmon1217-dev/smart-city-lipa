## 🎬 Demo Scenario Quick Guide

### Scenario: AI Evacuation Route

**Setup:** User needs to evacuate due to flood

**Steps:**
1. Show map with flood risk (red areas)
2. Open AI Advisor
3. Ask: "Should I evacuate?"
4. AI responds: "Yes, evacuate now..."
5. Click "Show Evacuation Route"
6. Show color-coded route (green/yellow/red)
7. Explain: Green = safe, Yellow = caution, Red = dangerous
8. Show route details: distance, time, risk level
9. Click "Start Navigation"

**Time:** 2-3 minutes

**Key Points:**
- AI provides personalized advice
- Visual route risk display
- Safety-first routing
- Integration with navigation
